# QPX Alpha Quant Research Platform

## Overview

QPX Alpha is a modular quantitative research and trading platform designed to provide an end-to-end framework for researching, validating, and simulating systematic trading strategies.

The platform is built around a staged validation process covering:

- Market data ingestion
- Data validation
- Feature engineering
- Signal generation
- Backtesting
- Portfolio construction
- Risk management
- Execution simulation
- Performance attribution
- Walk-forward testing
- Stress testing
- Production readiness
- Final platform certification

The goal of QPX Alpha is to create a professional-grade research environment where trading ideas can be developed, tested, validated, and analyzed before deployment.
